Path of Legends Autopost

Path of Legends Autopost is a private publishing tool used by the Path of Legends channel to publish the short-form videos it creates to its own Facebook Page, YouTube channel and TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator finishes a short vertical video and places it, with its title and caption, in their own Google Drive folder.
  2. At the time the creator has scheduled, Path of Legends Autopost uploads that video to the creator's own accounts using the official APIs (YouTube Data API, Facebook Graph API, TikTok Content Posting API).
  3. Each platform receives the caption and privacy setting the creator chose for that video.

The tool does not read, collect or post anything from any other user, and it is not offered to the public.
