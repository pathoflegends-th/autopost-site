Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Path of Legends Autopost ("the App") is operated by Path of Legends for publishing the channel's own videos. This policy explains what data the App uses.

Data we access

  • Google Drive: only the files in the folder the owner designates for publishing (videos and their caption files).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload). We do not read viewer data.
  • Facebook: a Page access token for the owner's Page, used only to publish Reels.

How we use it

Data is used solely to upload the owner's videos to the owner's accounts. We do not sell, share, rent or use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and in the owner's own Google Drive. Videos are downloaded temporarily during upload and deleted when the job ends. A log of published post IDs is kept in the owner's Drive.

Third parties

Video files and captions are sent only to Google/YouTube and Meta/Facebook through their official APIs. If the optional caption feature is enabled, the video's title and caption text (not the video) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft platform-specific captions.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party access and Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. To request deletion of any stored data, email titititletiktok@gmail.com; it will be deleted within 30 days.

Google API Services

Path of Legends Autopost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
